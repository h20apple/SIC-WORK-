Record for:
Despatch Short Interval Control (SIC) Sheet
Greencore®
Reason for Issue: New document issued
Purpose: Real-time monitoring of production output, order fulfilment, mass balance the total number of packs against LOMA and OAL, and waste
Responsibility: Despatch Team leaders are responsible for completing the SIC sheet. Despatch Managers are responsible for reviewing performance and ensuring corrective actions are completed.
Procedure: Complete the SIC sheet at the defined monitoring intervals throughout the shift.
Retrieve the total number of reads from the Olympus system by running the Statistics Report after the production run has been completed and record the result in the OAL Total Reads field. Compare the results against packs produced and
identify any deviations, losses, quality failures, or delays.
Team Leader or Manager to verify the information and sign the sheet.
Corrective Action: If the number of OAL reads is lower than the number of packs produced, Despatch Manager must be informed, and issue investigated
